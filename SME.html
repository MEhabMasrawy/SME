Mathematical Architecture

Credit Sizing & Repayment Capacity Formula

Transparent Sizing Grounded in the 25% Monthly Retained Cash Rule

Deterministic Sizing Equations

1. Monthly Installment

25% of unencumbered monthly retained cash

Inst = 0.25 × B_left
2. Base Calculated Facility (12 Mos)

Annualized debt servicing capacity

Cap = Inst × 12 = 3.0 × B_left
3. Allowed Deal Limit (70% Rule)

Prudential haircut for downside protection

Offer = 0.70 × Cap = 2.10 × B_left
4. Program Upper Cap

RFP Phase 1 policy ceiling

Min(Offer, KD 100,000)
The 75% Liquidity Cushion

Why Borrowers Never Default

Because the offered deal is 70% of the calculated capacity, the borrower's actual debt service burden is only:

17.5% of monthly retained cash flow ($0.25 \times 0.70$)

The SME customer preserves 82.5% of their monthly liquidity cushion for supplier invoices and unforeseen operational fluctuations!

Fully compliant with Central Bank of Kuwait (CBK) micro/SME debt service ratio regulations.
